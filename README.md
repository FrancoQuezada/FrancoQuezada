# Franco Quezada

### Operations Research · Mathematical Optimization · Applied R&D · Decision Support

I develop **research-driven computational solutions for complex decision-making problems**, combining mathematical modeling, optimization algorithms, data, and software.

My work sits at the intersection of **Operations Research and applied R&D**: from identifying and formalizing real-world decision problems, to developing optimization methods and translating them into computational tools that can support better decisions.

I am particularly interested in problems involving **uncertainty, resource allocation, energy systems, production, logistics, and risk**.

## What I work on

* **Mathematical Optimization** — mixed-integer programming, decomposition methods, and large-scale optimization.
* **Stochastic Optimization** — decision-making under uncertainty, multistage models, and risk-aware optimization.
* **Algorithm Engineering** — specialized algorithms, bounds, heuristics, branch-and-bound, and decomposition approaches.
* **Decision Support Systems** — translating optimization models into computational tools for applied decision-making.
* **Applied R&D** — connecting research methods with real operational, industrial, environmental, and societal problems.

## Selected Projects

### 🔥 [Stochastic Firebreak Placement](https://github.com/FrancoQuezada/stochastic-firebreak-placement)

Risk-aware optimization for wildfire mitigation planning under uncertainty. The project studies where firebreaks should be placed under limited resources, combining stochastic optimization, CVaR, network models, and specialized decomposition algorithms.

### ⚡ [Multistage Stochastic Fair Allocation](https://github.com/FrancoQuezada/Multistage_stochastic_fair_allocation)

Multistage stochastic optimization for fair resource allocation in energy communities. The project integrates uncertainty, adaptive decision-making, and explicit fairness considerations into energy allocation decisions.

### 🧠 [Approximate Information-Reuse Branch-and-Bound](https://github.com/FrancoQuezada/Probing_Approximate_IR-B-B)

Algorithmic research on optimization problems involving information acquisition and repeated related subproblems, with emphasis on lower bounds, solution reuse, approximation, and specialized branch-and-bound methods.
